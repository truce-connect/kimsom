Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

Do you offer warranty on your products?

Yes, all our products come with manufacturer warranty. The warranty period varies by product type, typically ranging from 1-2 years. We honor all valid warranty claims and provide replacement or repair as per manufacturer guidelines.

Do you deliver outside Lagos Island?

Yes, we deliver to all parts of Lagos and other states in Nigeria. Delivery fees vary based on location and order size. For large orders, we may offer free delivery within certain areas.

Do you offer discounts for bulk purchases?

Absolutely! We offer special pricing for contractors, builders, and bulk purchasers. Contact us with your requirements and we'll provide you with a competitive quote tailored to your needs.

Are your products genuine and certified?

Yes, we guarantee that all our products are 100% genuine and sourced from authorized distributors. We only stock certified electrical products that meet international safety standards.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept multiple payment methods including bank transfers, cash, and mobile money transfers. For corporate clients, we also accept post-payment arrangements subject to credit approval.

Can I get technical advice for my project?

Yes, our experienced team provides free technical consultation to help you select the right products for your project. Visit our store or contact us for expert guidance on your electrical requirements.

About Kinmson Int'l Success Venture

My name is Olasehinde Lolade Segun, the Owner/Managing Director of Kinmson Int'l Success Venture, located at No. 24 Okoya Street, Off Adeniji Adele Road, Lagos Island, Lagos, Nigeria.

Kinmson Int'l Success Venture is a trusted supplier specializing in the wholesale and retail distribution of electrical and electronic products for homes, offices, and businesses. Our major products include light bulbs, rechargeable bulbs, extension sockets, and other general household electrical accessories.

Our company has been in operation for over 10 years, providing reliable and high-quality electrical products to customers across Lagos Island and other parts of Nigeria. Through our years of experience, we have built a strong reputation for quality products, affordable prices, and excellent customer service.

At Kinmson Int'l Success Venture, our goal is to provide durable electrical solutions that meet the needs of our customers while maintaining high standards of professionalism and integrity.

Over the years, we have successfully served many individuals, households, retailers, and contractors, helping them access dependable electrical products for their homes and projects.

To provide quality electrical products at competitive prices while ensuring maximum customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading suppliers of electrical and household electronic products in Nigeria.

We are committed to building long-term relationships with our customers by delivering reliable products, trusted service, and consistent value.
